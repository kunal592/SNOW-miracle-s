**character isn't just a cosmetic wallpaper**. It should become a visual representation of the user's progression.

I'd call this system **Evolution Theme**.

### The refined concept

The app has:

**Theme**

* Dark
* Light — **Minimal**
* Light — **Cinematic**

Then separately:

**Evolution Character**

The character/background changes as the AI Supervisor determines that the user's **actual behavior and progress** have evolved.

The key rule:

> **The AI never changes the character because of a single good day.**

It should require sustained evidence.

# 🧠 AI should determine evolution from evidence

This is the most important part.

Don't let the AI simply say:

> "You completed 5 goals → Level Up!"

Instead create an **Evolution Score** from multiple dimensions.

For example:

```text id="x8n1qm"
EVOLUTION PROFILE

Discipline           74
Consistency          68
Learning             81
Execution            62
Health               59
Financial Control    71
Time Management      64
Cognitive Growth     77
Goal Progress        73
```

Then the AI looks at:

* consistency over time
* milestone completion
* learning hours
* goal progress
* time management
* expense behavior
* cognitive challenge performance
* habit consistency
* journal/reflection patterns
* setbacks and recovery

**Not just one metric.**

# 🧩 Character stages

You could have something like:

### Stage 0 — Unknown

New user.

```text
Character:
silhouette / minimal image
```

The AI doesn't know enough yet.

---

### Stage 1 — Awakening

The user has established tracking consistency.

Criteria might include:

* 7+ days of meaningful tracking
* first goals established
* first milestones created
* first cognitive challenges completed

---

### Stage 2 — Discipline

Evidence of consistent behavior.

For example:

* 21+ days tracking
* improved time consistency
* learning routine established
* fewer abandoned goals

---

### Stage 3 — Growth

The user is actually progressing.

* goals moving
* milestones being completed
* learning increasing
* cognitive performance improving

---

### Stage 4 — Execution

The user consistently turns plans into actions.

* high milestone completion
* sustained learning
* stable routines
* better financial control
* fewer unplanned time leaks

---

### Stage 5 — Mastery

Not "you're perfect."

Instead:

> **Your systems are working consistently.**

The AI should explicitly explain why the user reached the stage.

# 🔒 Evolution should have safeguards

This is important.

The AI should **not** change the character automatically every time it thinks:

> "You're doing better."

Instead:

```text
Candidate Evolution
       ↓
Minimum observation period
       ↓
Data quality check
       ↓
AI analysis
       ↓
Evolution confidence
       ↓
Unlock
```

Example:

```text
Current Stage: Discipline

Candidate:
Growth

Evidence period:
28 days

Confidence:
91%

Reason:
Learning consistency +32%
Goal completion +21%
Tracking consistency +18%

Status:
Ready for evolution
```

Then show the user:

> **You've evolved.**
>
> Your behavior over the last 28 days shows sustained improvement in learning consistency, goal completion and time management.
>
> **New evolution unlocked.**

---

# 🧠 The AI should also be allowed to say "No evolution"

This is actually important.

At the monthly review:

> **Evolution Review**
>
> Your overall performance improved, but the improvement wasn't consistent enough to qualify for the next stage.
>
> Your strongest area was learning.
>
> Your biggest inconsistency was time management.
>
> Current stage remains: **Discipline**

That is much more meaningful than blindly rewarding the user.
